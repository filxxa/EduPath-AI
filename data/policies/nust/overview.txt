# NUST — Overview

Full name: National University of Sciences and Technology.
Location: Islamabad.
Website: https://nust.edu.pk.

## Description

Multi-school university offering undergraduate programs in engineering, computing, business, social sciences, law, architecture, natural and applied sciences.

## Disclaimer

This dataset is a decision-support corpus, not official university verification. Where an institution did not publish a single program-specific numeric threshold/cutoff/date in the sources reviewed, the field is left null or a conservative baseline is used only where the institution publishes a general minimum. Always confirm the final application details on the official university portal.
