# UET Lahore — Overview

Full name: University of Engineering and Technology, Lahore.
Location: Lahore.
Website: https://uet.edu.pk.

## Description

Engineering and technology university with a large Lahore catalog plus programmes at KSK, Faisalabad, Narowal and RCET Gujranwala.
