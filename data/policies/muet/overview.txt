# MUET — Overview

Full name: Mehran University of Engineering and Technology.
Location: Jamshoro.
Website: https://www.muet.edu.pk.

## Description

Public-sector engineering and technology university with a broad Jamshoro undergraduate catalog and SZAB Campus Khairpur Mir’s offerings.
